API 사용방법
  • End point : {{current_url}}/utilities/api
  • Method : POST
  • Header에 발급된 Token 전송
    • Voronoi-Token : 발급받은 Token
  • 전송 parameter
    • t : api호출 타입
      호출 타입 설명 비고
      solubility Solubility calculation. 전송 parameter에 u 추가 전달
      u값 : NM, UM, MM, LOGS, MGPERML, MOLPERL
      ※ 의미(nM, uM, mM, logS, mg/ml, mol/l)
      pka pKa calculation. temperature:298
      pka-dist pKa distribution calculation.
      logd log D calculation.
      logp log P calculation.
      charge Charge calculation. 전송 parameter에 p 추가 전달
      herg-act hERG Activity model calculation.
      herg-class hERG Classification model calculation.
      psa Polar surface area(PSA) calculation. 전송 parameter에 p 추가 전달
      hbda H-bond donors/acceptors caclulation. 전송 parameter에 p 추가 전달
      cns CNS MPO calculation
      elemental Elemental analysis calculation. 전송 parameter에 o 추가 전달(구분자 ,로 여러개 요청가능)
      tautomer-dominant Dominant-Tautomerization calculation.
    • s : smiles
    • p : ph (호출타입이 charge, psa, hbda인 경우만 사용)
    • u : unit (호출타입이 solubility인 경우만 사용)
    • o : operations (호출타입이 elemental인 경우만 사용)
      • mass: average molecular mass calculated from the standard atomic weights,
      • exactMass: monoisotopic mass calculated from the weights of the most abundant natural isotopes of the elements,
      • formula: chemical formula of the molecule according to the Hill system,
      • formalChargeFormula: chemical formula of the molecule including the formal charge according to the Hill system,
      • isotopeFormula: chemical formula of the molecule listing isotopes separately according to the Hill system,
      • isotopeFormalChargeFormula: chemical formula of the molecule listing isotopes separately and including the formal charge according to the Hill system,
      • dotDisconnectedFormula: chemical formula of the molecule(s) separating fragment formulas by dots (e.g. salts, counterions, solvent molecules etc. are present),
      • dotDisconnectedFormalChargeFormula: chemical formula of the molecule(s) separating fragment formulas by dots and including the formal charge (e.g. salts, counterions, solvent molecules etc. are present),
      • dotDisconnectedIsotopeFormula: chemical formula of the molecule separating fragment formulas by dots and listing isotopes separately,
      • dotDisconnectedIsotopeFormalChargeFormula: chemical formula of the molecule separating fragment formulas by dots, listing isotopes separately and including the formal charge,
      • groupedDotDisconnectedFormula: the grouped dot-disconnected molecular formula of a multifragment molecule,
      • composition: elemental composition given in weight percentage (w/w %) calculated from the atomic masses,
      • isotopeComposition: elemental composition listing isotopes separately (w/w %),
      • isotopeDistribution: the isotopic distributions for the molecule (the distribution of fragments are merged, it takes charge into account),
      • allAtomCount: number of all atoms in the molecule,
      • countAtoms: number of the specified atom in the molecule,
      • countIsotopes: number of the specified isotope in the molecule.
  • 응답
    • result_code
      code 설명
      0000 성공
      1111 토큰이 누락되었습니다.
      2222 토큰이 유효하지 않습니다.
      3333 호출 타입이 누락되었습니다.
      4444 smiles값이 누락되었습니다.
      5555 호출 횟수 제한에 걸려 더 이상 호출할 수 없습니다.
      6666 아직 구현되지 않는 호출 타입입니다.
      7777 ph값이 누락되었습니다.
      8888 unit값이 누락되었습니다.
      9000 operations값이 누락되었습니다.
      9999 전달한 데이터 중에 올바르지 않는 값이 있는 것으로 보입니다.
    • result_msg : 결과 메시지
    • result : 응답 결과 (json format)

      --header 'Voronoi-Token: 발급받은Token' \
